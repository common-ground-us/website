## Summary of the update

Common Ground's Platform selects policies meeting **three criteria**, not two. The third — *respect for individual constitutional rights as protected by the Bill of Rights* — has been present in our internal documentation (see *Civic Heart v8*) but is missing from the public-facing website copy and the Governance Principles subpage. This update fixes that.

The third criterion is a structural commitment, not a footnote. It's a constitutional constraint on what the Platform can include — even policies with overwhelming public support cannot abridge individual rights. This protects against the tyranny-of-the-majority concern that the Founders designed the Bill of Rights to address.

## The canonical three-criteria language

For reference — this language is now consistent across all three files:

> ### How the Platform Is Built
>
> The Common Ground Platform selects policies that meet three criteria:
>
> 1. **Supermajority public support** — a clear majority of Americans, well beyond a slim plurality, support the policy.
> 2. **Cross-partisan agreement** — that support holds across party lines, not just within one party.
> 3. **Respect for individual constitutional rights** — the policy operates within the limits the Bill of Rights places on government action.
>
> The third criterion is not a caveat. It is a constitutional constraint. A policy with overwhelming public support that would abridge a right protected by the Bill of Rights has no place on the Platform — however large the majority. American democracy was built on two foundational commitments: democratic accountability and individual liberty. The Platform honors both.

## Why this matters before launch

Without the third criterion, the public-facing copy reads as if CG would treat any policy with majority support as Platform-worthy — including policies that would violate constitutional rights. That's not what CG does, and it's not what we want communicated. The fix is mechanical, but it has to land before launch to set the framework correctly for the August Platform publication.
